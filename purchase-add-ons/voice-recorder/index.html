    Voice Recorder (add-on)

    The Voice Recorder add-on is a premium feature for the MysterSmith and PeekSmith apps (if you have already purchased the add-on for the PeekSmith app, you can use it in MysterSmith too, and don't have to buy the add-on again).

    The price of the add-on is currently 25 EUR (one time fee), but of course it's also included in both subscription plans (BASIC and PREMIUM).

    With the "Voice Recorder" feature you can record audio messages on your phone, and you can replay them at any time through any audio output (phone speaker, connected Bluetooth speaker, earpiece, or the Mental Wave bone conduction device by Electricks).

    Soon we will add the option to upload audio files from a website as well.

    Purchasing the "Voice recorder" add-on

    If you don't want to subscribe for MysterSmith (to get all the available add-ons), you can also purchase the Voice Recorder add-on for 25 EUR by clicking here:

    bsmagic.app/payment/?item=voicerecorder

    Activating your code

    If you are a MysterSmith subscriber, the add-on is already activated for you.

    If you have purchased the add-on separately, or received the code as a gift, you can activate the code by entering the code in MysterSmith, in the ACCOUNT STTINGS section. Just look for the line "Do you have a code?", and "Tap to enter", and the code activation screen will pop up.

    After the code is activated, you can check all your activated add-ons on your account details page. Just tap "Show details" under ACCOUNT SETTINGS, and scroll down to the PURCHASED ADD-ONS section. If you have an active MysterSmith subscription, you will have access to all the available add-ons.
