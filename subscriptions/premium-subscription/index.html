    PREMIUM subscription

    PREMIUM subscription  - price plans

    Retail price:

    1 month = 45 EUR

    20% discount for the quarterly plan:

    3 months = 108 EUR (36 EUR/month)

    How to subscribe?

    Please sign in to the Account Manager with the same account as you use on your phone to sign in to the MysterSmith app.

    What’s included?

    As long as your PREMIUM subscription is active, you will get:

    • Limited to 50 subscribers (there will be a wait list in case all "seats" are taken)
    • Access to all features/routines/add-ons in MysterSmith(*)
    • Access to exclusive features available to subscribers only
    • Early access for some new features, ahead of the official release
    • Access to ALL my apps and add-ons (worth 1235 EUR) (*)
    • Unlimited text recognition credits (to be used with the Google engine in any of my apps)
    • Access to my services at https://mkm.mom (to be released in 2025)
    • Access to my Messenger trick called Mental Agency (to be released in 2025)
    • Access to the Mindreader Alexa skill (to be released in 2025)
    • 50% discount for certain 3rd party add-ons (sold by other developers, purchased through my service)
    • 50% discount for credit-based extra services (like HeyGen video avatar creation)
    • Access to the closed Facebook group of MysterSmith subscribers
    • Access to the private helpdesk chat for MysterSmith subscribers (to be released later in 2025)
    • Support of unofficially supported hardware in all my apps
    • Support of every possible magic hardware in all my apps (if I can add support)

    Make sure you check out the upcoming new featuresas well: click here

    Rewards after 3 months of continuous subscription:(starting with the payment for the 4th month)

    • Locked subscription fee (even if I officially raise the monthly fee)
    • Option to sell/transfer app and add-on licences to another user (if purchased separately before subscribing)
    • 50% discount for purchasing my apps and add-ons (if you'd like to get lifetime access)
    • Option to pause the subscription two times (without loosing rewards), for a maximum time of 3 months/pause

    (*)Paid services that require additional work from me (like prediction photo editing) are not included. Credit-based extra services depending on usage (like HeyGen video avatar creation) are not included. 3rd party add-ons (like the the Dictionary API add-on by Ben Laxton) are NOT included.**

    Downgrading to the BASIC plan

    You can only downgrade your PREMIUM plan to the BASIC plan at the start of the next billing cycle. When downgrading, no refund will be received (as you use your PREMIUM subscription for the whole paid period).

    You can choose between the 1 month or 3 months cycle for the downgraded BASIC plan. If this is the first time you subscribe for the BASIC plan, you will get the 20% discount offer if you select the 3 month billing cycle. In case you already had a BASIC subscription before, you can only sign up at the retail price.

    Regarding rewards after a 3-month continuous subscription, when downgrading from PREMIUM to BASIC, you will get to keep your rewards such as the locked subscription fee and the option to sell purchased app licenses. However, you won't be able to pause your subscription until the 4th continuous month of your BASIC plan begins.
