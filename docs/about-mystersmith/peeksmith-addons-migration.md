# PeekSmith add-ons & Electricks Cloud

!!! tip "In short"
    **In MysterSmith NOTHING changes.** Your add-ons keep working exactly as before.

    This page is only about the **PeekSmith app**: if you would like to keep using your add-ons **there**,
    migrate them to your **Electricks Cloud** account with your personal **BSMAGIC code**.

---

## Why is this needed?

* **Until 2025** I sold add-ons for the **PeekSmith app** (External Displays, Audio Assistant, Voice Recorder, Send to API, WEB-doodle...).
* **In 2025** the **MysterSmith** app was released. The add-ons you bought for the PeekSmith app worked in MysterSmith too - but the newer add-ons are available in MysterSmith only.
* **In summer 2025** the development of the **PeekSmith app** was taken over by **Electricks**. The PeekSmith app is their app now, and they manage its add-ons in their own system, called **Electricks Cloud**.
* **From October 2026**, the add-ons you bought from me can be used in the **PeekSmith app** only if you **migrate them to Electricks Cloud**.

**MysterSmith is not affected:** your add-ons and your subscription keep working in MysterSmith, whether you migrate them or not.

---

## Which add-ons can be migrated?

The add-ons that worked in the PeekSmith app:

* **External Displays**
* **Audio Assistant**
* **Voice Recorder**
* **Send to API**
* **WEB-doodle**

Bundles count too: the Multi-Display add-on (External Displays + Audio Assistant + Send to API), the MysterSmith Essential Tools (External Displays + Send to API), and API Access / A.I. Features (Send to API).

**Active BASIC and PREMIUM subscribers** can also use the add-ons above that they haven't bought separately, in the PeekSmith app **until 31st December 2026**.

---

## How to migrate

You have a **personal code**, like this: **BSMAGIC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX**. You can find it in three places:

1. **In the e-mail** I sent you - just click the **Redeem on Electricks Cloud** button.
2. In the **[BS Magic Account Manager](https://bsmagic.app/account/){:target="_blank"}**, in the "PeekSmith add-ons on Electricks Cloud" section.
3. In the **MysterSmith app**: Settings &rarr; ACCOUNT SETTINGS &rarr; **Show details**, in the "PEEKSMITH ADD-ONS" block (tap the code to copy it).

Then open the redeem page (the button does it for you), sign in to your **Electricks Cloud** account (or create one), and redeem the code. That's all: your add-ons will be available in the PeekSmith app.

The redeem page: **https://cloud.electricks.info/redeem/** + your code.

## Questions

**I use MysterSmith only. Do I have to do anything?**<br>
No. Nothing changes in MysterSmith.

**I didn't get the e-mail.**<br>
Check your spam folder. If it's not there, sign in to the [BS Magic Account Manager](https://bsmagic.app/account/){:target="_blank"} or open the account details in the MysterSmith app (ACCOUNT SETTINGS &rarr; Show details): if you have add-ons that can be migrated, your code is shown there.

**I don't see a code anywhere.**<br>
Then there are no PeekSmith add-ons on your BS Magic account (for example, you bought them on another account, or after 1st August 2026). If you think this is a mistake, [contact me](https://mystersmith.info/contact/contact/).

**Can I keep using the add-ons in MysterSmith after migrating them?**<br>
Yes. Migrating only makes them available in the PeekSmith app as well - nothing is taken away from MysterSmith.
