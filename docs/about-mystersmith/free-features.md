# Free features for everyone

# What can you do with the FREE app?

### (without purchasing add-ons or a subscription)

  
Good news: many features in MysterSmith are **FREE** for everyone!!! :-)  
  
  
**DISPLAYING DATA:**  
  

* you can display received text on the BLACK SCREEN
* you can display handwritings, signatures, playing cards, magic squares, Tic-Tac-Toe boards etc. on the Doodle screen
* you can display text, drawing and images on the PeekSmith 3 and Bond devices (no add-on required until the end of 2025)
* you can display text on the SB Watch 2 (no add-on required until the end of 2025)
* you can display images, handwritings, playing cards, magic squares, ESP symbols etc. on Teleport (no add-on required until the end of 2025)
* you can display text and drawings on the MrCard device (no add-on required until the end of 2025)

  
  
**RECEIVING DATA:**  
  

* WEB-polling: you can receive info from any WEB API (like Inject, WikiTest, Elips, Topix, Goo etc. or any other source)
* Custom URL scheme: you can receive data from any app through the mystersmith:// scheme
* HTTP Socket: you can receive data from any app or WEB-browser (on the same local WiFi network) on localhost:62369
* WEB-input (WEB-thumper): you can receive data from any WEB-browser, from anywhere in the world (planet Earth)
* manual input: your assistant can enter text/numbers, playing cards, colors etc. manually
* you can use the built-in NFC reader of your phone to receive data
* you can use supported NFC readers to receive data
* on Android you can catch and display notifications from any 3rd party app
* you can view words (and point values) from the Scrabble device by Labco
* you can display numbers coming from the Quantum calculator

  
  
**IMPRESSION BOARDS:**  
  

* you can display handwriting and drawings from impression boards on the Doodle screen and on the BLACK SCREEN

  
  
**HANDWRITING RECOGNITION:**  
  

* you get 250 free recognition credits, to be used with the Google Vision recognition engine (one recognition = 1 credit)

  
  
**CONTROLLING THE APP:**  
  

* you can use Volume buttons (and bluetooth shutter remotes that simulate a volume button press) to trigger any available action in the app
* you can tap the corners on impression board devices to trigger any available action in the app
* you can use the buttons of the PeekSmith 3 and Bond devices to trigger any available action in the app (no add-on required until the end of 2025)
* you can use the buttons of the Quantum calculator to trigger some actions in the app (no add-on required until the end of 2025)

  
  
**TESTING E-INK DEVICES AND PRINTERS:**  
  

* you can connect any supported e-ink device and printer, and send a TEST image/text to see if they work properly with the app
