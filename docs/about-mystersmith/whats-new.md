# What's new in MysterSmith v2.3.71

Since **v2.3.61**, MysterSmith prints in **color**: HP Sprocket, Polaroid, Canon, LG and Liene photo printers turn your Image Buffer pictures, last photos and image templates into real photos. There is a **free bonus music quiz**, a new settings panel for the **Iarvel Peek Board / Spirit Echo**, smarter **Quickdraw** drawings in any language, and your add-ons are now protected by a **signed license** on max. 3 devices. Here are the highlights. :-)

---

## New: Color photo printers

A new device in **MY DEVICES**: the **Color photo printer**. It prints full-color photos on ZINK / photo paper:

| Printer | Android | iOS |
|---|---|---|
| HP Sprocket (1st edition / Plus) | yes | yes |
| Polaroid ZIP / Mint | yes | – |
| Canon IVY / Zoemini | yes | – |
| LG PD233 | yes | – |
| Liene Pearl | yes | yes |

New actions in the *Color photo printer* group:

* **image buffer to color photo printer** — an A.I. drawing, a photo from WEB-polling, anything in the [Image Buffer](../user-guide/image-buffer.md)
* **last photo to color photo printer**
* **doodle drawing to color photo printer**
* **image template to color photo printer** — a new template format, *Color photo printer (1254 x 1881)*, to design a full photo with your own background, images and texts (subscribers - see [Image templates](../user-guide/image-templates.md))

Plus an **Action after printing finished** trigger. The color photo printer needs the [Printers add-on](../purchase-add-ons/printers.md) or a subscription.

## New: MUSIC GAME (free bonus)

A music quiz for any number of players — in **Settings → INTEGRATIONS → MUSIC GAME** (at the bottom):

1. Type a **topic** ("Hits of the 80s", "Movie soundtracks", "Eurovision winners"...) and choose the **number of songs** (5–50).
2. **Create song list**: the A.I. picks the songs (Gemini, OpenAI or Claude).
3. **START GAME**: the songs come one by one with a 30-second preview. The **year**, the **artist**, the **title** (and the **film** for a soundtrack) are hidden — tap one to reveal it.

The rules are up to you: guess the title, the artist or the year, play in teams, give points...

* After an **EXIT**, START GAME asks: continue from the same song, or shuffle and start again. After the last song a new round starts in a new random order.
* **Subscribers** play with the [free Gemini calls](../subscriptions/free-gemini.md). Without a subscription, the [A.I. Features add-on](../purchase-add-ons/ai-features.md) and your own Gemini, OpenAI or Claude API key are needed.

## Iarvel Peek Board / Spirit Echo

* **A new settings panel:** MY DEVICES → *Settings* opens it like for every other device — basic settings, image sending options, tests and display customization in one place. (The old "Device settings" block of the Settings page is gone.)
* **Add fake dust effect:** a new switch for chalk mode, like the Inversion Board's. Switch it off for a clean drawing (on by default).

---

## Quickdraw

**Settings → INTEGRATIONS → QUICKDRAW** (after TrueWrite / TrueSketch):

* **Translate to English using A.I.** — the Quickdraw drawings have English names. With this switch on, the spectator's word is first translated from your **Performance language** and matched to the closest drawing (e.g. *"kutya"* → *dog*) — on the Doodle screen **and** on the Peek Board. It uses the *Engine for translations* of A.I. FEATURES (your own key, or the free Gemini calls of the subscribers).
* **Action after drawing generated** — Quickdraw has its own trigger now (before, it fired the *Action after OpenAI drawing* one).

More: [A.I. drawings → Quickdraw](../user-guide/ai-drawings.md#quickdraw).

## Your add-ons on max. 3 devices

Your add-ons and your subscription are now confirmed by a **signed license** from our server, valid on max. **3 devices** of your account at the same time:

* **Account details → DEVICES** lists your devices. A new device says *"You have activated device 2 of 3 on your account."*
* On a 4th device the add-ons are switched off, and a message lists your other devices: **Sign out** one of them right there. Signing out of the app frees the device too, and a device not used for **60 days** is signed out by itself.
* The license works **offline** too, for 30 days after the last connection — your shows never depend on the Internet.
