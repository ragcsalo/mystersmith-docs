# Upcoming features and routines

I already have a ton of **ideas for new features and routines**, and I'm sure this list will grow as time flies by... And don't forget: most of these features/routines will be **available as paid add-ons**, but of course almost all of them will be  **included in both subscription plans** (except 3rd party add-ons, sold by other developers) - but some of them will be **exclusive for PREMIUM subscribers** for at least a month before released for the public. 
 

---

 
 **New features**  - some of which I've been planning to add for 3 years now: 
 

* Routine playlist: perform more routines without the need of tampering with settings
* Saving prediction photos (locally and online)
* A.I. video avatar predictions/revelations
* A.I. music predictions/revelations
* Dark Doodle screen (white on black, no toolbars)
* Dynamic text (like playing card to time, word to playing card etc.)
* QR code predictions (scan and reveal)
* Augmented Reality features (revelation through the camera)
 

* **3rd party add-ons** (the only one that's not included in subscriptions)

 
 
...and many-many more to come. 
 
 
Stay tuned!
