Here is everything you need to know about this transition, what it means for your active subscription, and what steps (if any) you need to take.

* 🟢 **Your subscription is still ACTIVE and is NOT cancelled.**

## Detailed Transition Information

### 1. PayPal Automatic Payments Cancelled (But Subscriptions Stay Active!)
To facilitate the transfer of our system, we have cancelled the automatic recurring payments on our old PayPal account. 
* **Important:** This only cancels the automatic billing agreement on PayPal. **Your actual MysterSmith subscription itself remains fully active** and valid until the end of your current billing period. 
* If the transition takes more time and cannot be completed before your current subscription period ends, **you will receive FREE extra days** to ensure your service remains uninterrupted and active.

### 2. Upgrading to Paddle.com
Once the new company system goes live, all future subscription payments will be processed through **Paddle.com** (our new Merchant of Record). 
* **Supported Payment Methods:** Paddle will allow you to subscribe using **PayPal, Credit Cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay**.
* **Price Lock Guarantee:** When the new system is active, we will notify you immediately. All current subscribers will have **at least 2 weeks (14 days)** to re-subscribe via Paddle.com at your **exact same historical price**, ensuring you do not lose your continuous subscriber status or face price increases.
