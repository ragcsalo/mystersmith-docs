## Changing your plan

You can upgrade your BASIC plan to the PREMIUM plan **at any time** in the [Account Manager](https://bsmagic.app/account/), and choose the 1 month or 3 months cycle for the PREMIUM plan. You can also switch your monthly BASIC plan to **quarterly billing** the same way. 
 
You'll be charged the **prorated difference** right away (the price of the new plan for the rest of your current billing period, minus the unused part of your current fee). If the billing cycle stays the same, your renewal date doesn't change; if it changes, you'll see your new renewal date before you confirm. From your next renewal, the full price of the new plan applies. 
 
Downgrading to the MINI plan, or switching from quarterly to monthly billing, isn't possible during an active subscription. Cancel your subscription in the [Account Manager](https://bsmagic.app/account/) (it stays active until the end of the paid period), and once it has ended, subscribe to the new plan. 
 
 
 **Regarding rewards** after a 3-month continuous subscription, when upgrading from BASIC to PREMIUM, your **3-month period will start over**, and you will temporarily loose your rewards (such as the locked subscription fee, option to sell purchased app licenses or pause the subscription).
