## Upgrading to the PREMIUM plan

You can upgrade your BASIC plan to the PREMIUM plan **at any time** during your active subscription period, or starting at the next billing cycle.  
  
If you decide to do the upgrade **during your paid period** of the BASIC plan, the remaining period will be refunded (total paid fee / total days in period \* remaining days). You will receive the partial refund after you signed up for the PREMIUM plan, and paid your first fee. If you choose to switch your plan starting **at your next billing cycle**, no refund will be received (as you use your BASIC subscription for the whole paid period).  
  
In any case, you can choose between the 1 month or 3 months cycle for the PREMIUM plan. If this is the first time you subscribe for the PREMIUM plan, you will get the **20% discount offer** if you select the 3 month billing cycle. In case you already had a PREMIUM subscription before, you can only sign up at the retail price.  
  
**Regarding rewards** after a 3-month continuous subscription, when upgrading from BASIC to PREMIUM, your **3-month period will start over**, and you will temporarily loose your rewards (such as the locked subscription fee, option to sell purchased app licenses or pause the subscription).
