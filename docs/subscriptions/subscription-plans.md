
# Subscription plans

## How to subscribe?

Please  **[sign in to the Account Manager](https://bsmagic.app/account/)** with the same account as you use on your phone to sign in to the MysterSmith app. 
 

---

 

### New: turn your subscription fee into credits!

If you keep your subscription active for at least 1 year (without pausing), and you ever decide to cancel the subscription, you can turn your paid fee into credits, and purchase any add-ons and apps you don't already own! 
 
[Click here for more details...](https://mystersmith.info/subscriptions/credits-reward/)
