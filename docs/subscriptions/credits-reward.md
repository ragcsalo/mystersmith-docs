# Credits reward

# Turn your subscription fee into credits!

If you keep your subscription **active for at least 1 year** (without pausing), and you **ever decide to cancel the subscription**, you can **turn your paid fee into credits**, and purchase **any add-ons and apps****that were included in your subscription**, but you don't already own!  
  

* If you cancel your subscription **after 1 year** of continuous subscription, you can turn **50%** of the total paid fee into credits.
* If you cancel your subscription **after 1.5 years** of continuous subscription, you can turn **75%** of the total paid fee into credits.
* If you cancel your subscription **after 2 years** of continuous subscription, you can turn **100%** of the total paid fee into credits.
* If I ever decide to terminate the subscription model, you can turn 100% of the total paid fee of your currently active, continuous subscription into credits.

  
You can **use these credits** to purchase any **add-ons and/or apps that were included in your subscription, but you don't own yet**, at the actual retail price. The time limit for using your credits will match the duration of your continuous subscription.  
  
Your credits can be only used for purchasing add-ons and apps for your own account.  
  

---

  

## Example #1

You've been paying your **BASIC subscription** fees (**48 EUR every 3 months**) continuously for 1 year (without pausing). Then after 1 year you decide not to renew the subscription, and cancel it. During the continuous subscription you've paid **4 x 48 = 192 EUR** fee, and you can **turn 50% of this fee into 96 credits** (=96 EUR). You can **use your credits to buy** some apps and/or add-ons that were included in your BASIC subscription, but you haven't purchased before...  
  
For example:  
  
Printers add-on (45 EUR)  
Audio assistant add-on (15 EUR)  
External displays add-on (35 EUR)  
  
**TOTAL: 95 EUR**  
  
The **time limit** for using your credits for purchasing **will be 1 year** (matching the duration of your last continuous subscription).
