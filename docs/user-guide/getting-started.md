# Getting started

MysterSmith connects your phone to your **peek devices, impression boards, printers, remotes and smartwatches**, and moves information between them during a performance: a spectator writes a word, the app recognizes it, transforms it (a template, an A.I. prompt, a calculation...) and sends it to the device where you — or the spectator — see it.

Most of it is set up once, at home, in **Settings**. On stage you just load your **routine** and tap **START ROUTINE**. This page walks you through the first steps.

## 1. Sign in

Open the app and sign in with **Apple** or **Google**. Your account keeps your routines, your Text and Image Buffer, your templates and your purchased add-ons — on every phone you sign in on.

After signing in, the home screen shows:

* **START ROUTINE** — starts the performance (see step 6), with the name of the active routine under it,
* **Create/Load routine**,
* **EXIT APP**,
* at the top: your **Account ID** and the **Network** state; at the bottom: **Home**, **START**, **User Guide** and **Settings**.

!!! tip "Do you have a code?"
    If you received a code (a gift, a device bundle, a voucher), open **Settings → ACCOUNT SETTINGS → Do you have a code? → Tap to enter**. Your account details (credentials, subscription, purchased add-ons, your MysterSmith API key) are under **ACCOUNT SETTINGS → Show details**.

---

## 2. Set your username and PIN

Besides the Apple / Google sign-in, MysterSmith needs a **BS Magic username and PIN**. They are needed for **text recognition**, **subscribing**, the **free Gemini A.I. calls**, and the web features where you log in from a browser (e.g. the **WEB screen**). Until they are set, a red reminder shows under CREDENTIALS in your account details.

1. Open **Settings → ACCOUNT SETTINGS → Show details**.
2. In the **CREDENTIALS** part, tap **Username** and enter any username that is not taken yet: lowercase letters and numbers, no spaces. If you already have a BS Magic username (from another BS Magic app), use that one, with its PIN.
3. Tap **PIN code** and enter a 4–6 digit number.

!!! tip "Guided tour"
    The app can show you the way: open the **User Guide** tab (bottom bar) → **Guided tours** → **Account setup: username & PIN**. The tour highlights each step on your screen.

---

## 3. Find your way in Settings

The Settings page is long — these help:

* **Search settings** at the top: type a word (e.g. *volume*, *printer*, *template*) and only the matching settings remain.
* **Jump chips** under it: **Top**, **Routines**, **Devices**, **Actions**, **Input**, **Transform**, **Output**, **More**.
* The page follows the way information moves through the app:

| Group | What you find there |
|---|---|
| **TEXT BUFFER / IMAGE BUFFER** | everything the app received, see [Text Buffer](text-buffer.md) and [Image Buffer](image-buffer.md) |
| **MY ROUTINES** | save / load your settings, see [Routines](routines.md) |
| **MY DEVICES** | connect and set up your Bluetooth devices |
| **MY ACTIONS** | what happens on the volume buttons, the app logo, after the app starts, device buttons..., see [Triggers and actions](triggers-and-actions.md) |
| **A.I. FEATURES** | your A.I. engines and API keys |
| **Input methods** | text recognition, volume buttons, WEB-polling, dice, remote controls, voice recognition, fake passcode, calculator, swipe, Morse, NFC... |
| **Transform / process** | templates, A.I. text and image prompts, date calculator, acronym word list |
| **Output / screens** | Send to API, prediction photo, image templates, watches, Doodle screen, black screen, fake homescreen, external displays, audio... |
| **Device settings** | Peek Board, Reinkstone |
| **More settings** | integrations, font manager, other settings, debug console |

Tap a section's title to open it.

---

## 4. Connect your devices

Open **MY DEVICES**:

1. **Enable/disable BT devices → Manage** shows every supported device. Switch on the ones you own (**ENABLE ALL** / **Disable all** at the top; **long press** a device to make it a favorite, and **Show only favorites** to keep the list short).
2. Turn on your device and tap **Connect** next to it. Once it is connected, **Settings** opens the device's own panel: device info, basic settings, a test, and its button actions.
3. **Auto-connect devices** decides what connects by itself when the app starts: *All nearby devices*, *My saved devices*, *Saved for this routine* or *Custom (per device setting)*.

The full list: [Supported devices](supported-devices.md).

---

## 5. Hide what you don't need

MysterSmith has a lot of features. Show only yours: **MY ROUTINES → Enable/disable features → Manage**. Switch off what you don't use (**Disable all**, then switch on the few you need), and **long press** to mark favorites. Hidden features disappear from the Settings page; switch them on again any time.

---

## 6. Make your first routine

1. Set up one effect: e.g. **text recognition** on the Doodle screen, and **MY ACTIONS → Volume UP → show last text**.
2. **MY ROUTINES → SAVE AS NEW**, give it a name (e.g. *Drawing duplication*).
3. Go back to the home screen and tap **START ROUTINE**.

**START ROUTINE** opens the performance screen: big buttons for the screens and input methods you enabled (**TEXT/NUMBERS, CARDS, COLORS, DOODLE, BLACK SCREEN, DICE SCREEN, SWIPE INPUT, MORSE INPUT, HOMESCREEN, PASSCODE, CALCULATOR, THUMBWRITER, PLAY AUDIO...**) and switches for the background inputs (**CLIPBOARD, WEB-POLLING, VOICE RECOGN., NFC READER, WEB-INPUT, WEB-DOODLE, LAST PHOTO**). It also saves your settings automatically and clears the buffers / screens if you asked for it.

!!! note "Save your changes"
    Changing a setting does not change the routine until you tap **SAVE** in MY ROUTINES. More: [Routines](routines.md).
