# The WEB-screen

The **WEB-screen** shows the texts sent from MysterSmith on a web page, in any browser: on a second phone, a tablet or a laptop. It is **free** (no add-on needed, since v2.3.94). It was rebuilt in **November 2026**: it is a modern, responsive page now, and it can be installed as a **fullscreen app**.

It works with my other apps too (PeekSmith, TimeSmith, CubeSmith, DiceSmith ...): pick the app when you log in.

## Getting started

1. In MysterSmith switch on **Enable WEBscreen** (Settings → Output / Screens → **OUTPUT OPTIONS**).
2. On the other device open [bsmagic.app/webscreen](https://bsmagic.app/webscreen/?app=mystersmith){:target="_blank"}.
3. Log in with your **User ID** and **PIN code** (the same as in the app), and pick the **Magic app**.

From now on every new text of the app appears on the WEB-screen. The browser remembers the login, so next time it starts at once.

## Settings

**Triple-tap** anywhere on the screen, or tap the faint **gear** in the bottom left corner, to open the settings:

* **Display** — **Fullscreen** (black screen, big text) or **Small peek screen** (a small 300×150 frame at the **top**, in the **middle** or at the **bottom** of the screen; the frame line can be switched off).
* **Text size**, **Text color** and **Opacity**. You can also **swipe up / down** on the screen to make the text bigger / smaller. A long text is made smaller automatically, so that it fits.
* **Playing cards** — a card (like `4H`, `4♥`, `queen of spades` or `KH AS 7C`) is shown as a **card face**, or as a **symbol** (4♥, with a red heart).
* **Touch to peek** — the text is shown only **while you touch the screen** (the rest of the time the screen is all black).
* **Vertical phone only** — the text is shown only while the phone is **held upright** (on iPhone, the page asks for the motion sensor permission when you switch it on).
* **Hide peek after** — new data is shown only for **this many seconds**, then the screen goes black until the next text (0 = Off).
* **Keep screen awake** — the screen does not turn off while the WEB-screen is open.
* **Show settings gear** — the faint gear in the corner (on by default; triple-tap always works).
* **Show a sample** — try your settings with a sample text and card, without sending anything from the app.
* **Log out** — to log in with another user or another app.

The settings are stored in the browser of that device.

!!! tip "Fullscreen"
    Installed on the home screen (see below), the WEB-screen starts as a fullscreen app. In a normal browser tab on Android, it switches to fullscreen at your first tap. On an iPhone the status bar (time, battery) can not be hidden by a web page — hold the phone in **landscape** if you need a completely black screen (iOS hides the status bar then).

## Fullscreen app shortcut on iOS (Safari)

1. Open [bsmagic.app/webscreen](https://bsmagic.app/webscreen/?app=mystersmith){:target="_blank"} in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **View More**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**, then start the WEB-screen from its new icon on the home screen.

## Fullscreen app shortcut on Android (Chrome)

1. Open [bsmagic.app/webscreen](https://bsmagic.app/webscreen/?app=mystersmith){:target="_blank"} in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Install and create shortcut** (on some phones: *Add to Home screen*), then **Install**.
4. Start the WEB-screen from its new icon.
