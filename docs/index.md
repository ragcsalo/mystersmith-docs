# What is MysterSmith?

## What is MysterSmith?

  
 My name is Benke Smith, I'm a magic app developer from Hungary, and the creator of the "Smith" apps. The initial **idea of MysterSmith** was born in **May 2023**. As the PeekSmith app was evolving, I’ve added many features that were already beyond the app’s initial purpose (to be a companion app for the PeekSmith devices). I believed it was time for a brand new app that can offer everything the PeekSmith app does, and many-many more (including **proper routine management**, **unique routines**, and a much **wider support of magic hardware**). I’ve always planned to introduce a **subscription system** at some point, and MysterSmith seemed to be the right  tool for this.

### [Free features for everyone?? Click here to read on...](https://mystersmith.info/site/about-mystersmith/free-features/)

  
In February 2025 the MysterSmith app started as an evolved version of the PeekSmith app, which means it offered all the **features that the PeekSmith app had**(except firmware updates for Electricks hardware), and **all add-ons purchased for the PeekSmith app** continued working in MysterSmith the same way. Since then I've added **new features and routines** gradually, **based on user requests** and priorities. However, you will still need the PeekSmith app for firmware updates, even if you "switch" to MysterSmith. Also some exclusive features (like advanced Atom 2 functions) are only added to the PeekSmith app, which is now maintained by Electricks.  
  
**While most of the new features/routines **will also be available as a one-time purchase**, the subscription models will offer many-many more, especially for those who perform regularly. While the BASIC plan will be more than enough for most hobby-magicians, there is also a **PREMIUM plan** available (limited to 50 users!!!), intended **for professional magicians**.**
