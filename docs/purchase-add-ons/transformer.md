# Transformer add-on

The Transformer add-on is a premium feature for the MysterSmith app. The price of the add-on is currently **45 EUR** (one time fee), but of course it's also **included in both subscription plans** (BASIC and PREMIUM).   
  
 With this add-on you will be able to change some text inputs (recognition result, WEB-polling result etc.) to something else... currently the add-on offers these features:   
  

* create **templates** (inserting text from the Text Buffer)
* executing custom **A.I. prompts**

  
**Other features will be added** gradually during the coming weeks/months...  
  

---

  

### Templates

You can insert text that you received via any input method (text recognition, WEB-polling, swipe input, passcode input, NFC reader etc.) into pre-made templates, and display the result on connected devices (or send it to other apps).  
  
**Example:** *"In my dream I saw the #TXT1 in the hands of #TXT2."*  
  
A spectator picks any card and places it under the card box... another spectator writes the name of their favorite celebrity on a small notepad. Then you show them your handwritten prediction on a chalkboard: *"In my dream I saw the 7♠ in the hands of Natalie Portman."*

---

### A.I. Prompts

Similar to templates, you can insert text from the Text Buffer into pre-made prompts, and send it to an A.I. engine (GPT by OpenAI or Gemini by Google), and display the result on connected devices (or send it to other apps).  
  
**Example:** *"What is the zodiac sign of #TEXT? Please only reply the name of the zodiac, with all uppercase letters."*  
  
A spectator writes the name of their favorite celebrity on a small notepad (for example Brad Pitt). Then you show them your handwritten prediction on small, crumbled paper: *"SAGITTARUIS"*

---

### Purchasing the Transformer add-on

If you don't want to subscribe for MysterSmith (to get all the available add-ons), you can also purchase the Transformer add-on for **45 EUR** by clicking here:

**[bsmagic.app/payment/?item=transformer](https://bsmagic.app/payment/?item=transformer)**

### Activating your code

If you are a MysterSmith subscriber, the add-on is already activated for you.  
  
If you have purchased the add-on separately, or received the code as a gift, you can activate the code by entering the code in MysterSmith, in the **ACCOUNT STTINGS** section. Just look for the line "**Do you have a code?**", and "**Tap to enter**", and the code activation screen will pop up.  
  
After the code is activated, you can check all your activated add-ons on your account details page. Just tap "**Show details**" under ACCOUNT SETTINGS, and scroll down to the **PURCHASED ADD-ONS** section. If you have an active MysterSmith subscription, you will have access to all the available add-ons.
