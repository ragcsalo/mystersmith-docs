# External Displays (add-on)

> **IMPORTANT: add-on validity in the PeekSmith app**
>
> This add-on is for the MysterSmith app. However, if you have purchased the add-on before 1st August 2026, you can also use the add-on in the PeekSmith app (which is now owned/developed by Electricks Ltd.). BASIC and PREMIUM subscribers who already had an active subscription on 1st August 2026, can access the add-on in the PeekSmith app until 31st December 2026. For more info please visit the MysterSmith and PeekSmith facebook groups.

The External Displays add-on is a premium feature for the MysterSmith app (if you have previously purchased the add-on for the PeekSmith app, you can use it in MysterSmith too, and don't have to buy the add-on again). 
 
The price of the add-on is currently  **55 EUR**  (one time fee), but of course it's also  **included in all subscription plans**  (MINI, BASIC and PREMIUM). 
 
With the External Displays add-on activated, you'll be able to **display text from the MysterSmith app on a variety of 3rd party devices**, so you can enjoy most of the benefits of the app even if you don't have a PeekSmith, Bond or Teleport device.

### Activating your code

If you are a MysterSmith subscriber, the add-on is already activated for you. 
 
If you have purchased the add-on separately, or received the code as a gift, you can activate the code by entering it in MysterSmith, in the  **ACCOUNT STTINGS**  section. Just look for the line " **Do you have a code?** ", and " **Tap to enter** ", and the code activation screen will pop up. 
 
After the code is activated, you can check all your activated add-ons on your account details page. Just tap " **Show details** " under ACCOUNT SETTINGS, and scroll down to the  **PURCHASED ADD-ONS**  section. If you have an active MysterSmith subscription, you will have access to all the available add-ons.
