# WEB-doodle (add-on)

The WEB-doodle add-on is a premium feature for the MysterSmith app. The price of the add-on is currently  **55 EUR**  (one time fee), but of course it's also  **included in all subscription plans**  (MINI, BASIC and PREMIUM).

With the WEB-doodle add-on activated, the spectator can **draw or write anything on their own phone**, in a simple drawing page opened in the web browser - and you will **see the drawing** on your phone's screen and on your **peek devices**, while they are drawing.



---



### Features

* **Peek the drawing:** the spectator's drawing appears on the Doodle screen of your phone (in colors), and on your connected **peek devices**.
* **Handwriting recognition:** the app can **recognize handwritings** from the WEB-doodle, just like from the in-app Doodle screen, and **display the text** on your peek devices. With the [ **External Displays** ](https://mystersmith.info/purchase-add-ons/external-displays/) add-on you can see it on even more devices (like smartwatches), and with the [ **Audio Assistant** ](https://mystersmith.info/purchase-add-ons/audio-assistant/) add-on you can even **hear it** in your BT earpiece (text-to-speech). More info: [Text recognition](https://mystersmith.info/user-guide/text-recognition/).
* **Peek the selected color:** you can also peek the **color selected by the spectator** in the WEB-doodle: the same color is selected on your Doodle screen, and it is also shown on your peek devices (and on external displays, or spoken by the Audio Assistant, if you have those add-ons).
* **No app for the spectator:** the WEB-doodle is a simple web page, it works in any browser, on any phone.



---



### Setting it up

In the MysterSmith app go to the  **WEB-DOODLE**  settings, and turn on " **Enable WEB-doodle** ". The WEB-doodle uses your MysterSmith  **username**  (set in  **ACCOUNT SETTINGS** ), so make sure you have one.

There are two ways to connect the spectator's phone to your app:



#### 1. Username in the URL

Open the following URL on the spectator's phone (with your own username at the end):

<a href="https://webdoodle.ink" target="_blank" style="font-size: 1.5em !important; color: crimson;">webdoodle.ink/username</a>

The phone **remembers your username**, so the next time on the same phone it's enough to open  **webdoodle.ink**  (without the username). To make the phone forget it, open  **webdoodle.ink/reset** .



#### 2. The color sequence (no username in the URL)

In the  **WEB-DOODLE**  settings tap " **Set now** " near " **WEB-doodle color sequence** ". The Doodle screen starts: tap **any five colors quickly** (max. 1 second pause between the colors), and remember their order. The sequence can be like:

* red, green, green, yellow, blue
* green, green, yellow, yellow, red
* red, green, red, green, pink

If the color sequence is already reserved by another user, the app says so, and you'll have to enter a new one. If it's OK, the app confirms your new color sequence.

Then simply open  **webdoodle.ink**  on the spectator's phone, and **tap the same five colors** in the same order: the page connects to your app - no username needed at all.



---



### Performing the routine

On the  **routine page**  of the app tap the  **WEB-DOODLE**  button (it turns yellow while the WEB-doodle is running). The spectator can now draw or write anything on the WEB-doodle, and you will see it on your phone and on your peek devices. When the routine is over, tap the WEB-DOODLE button again to stop it.

More settings in the  **WEB-DOODLE**  section:

* **Autostart peek screen:** which screen starts with the WEB-doodle -  **Doodle screen** ,  **BLACK screen** ,  **always ask** , or  **nothing** .
* **Disable finger drawing:** your own finger doesn't draw on the peek screen (only the spectator's drawing is shown).
* **Peek selected color:** the color selected by the spectator is peeked too (see above).

### Activating your code

If you are a MysterSmith subscriber, the add-on is already activated for you.

If you have purchased the add-on separately, or received the code as a gift, you can activate the code by entering it in MysterSmith, in the  **ACCOUNT SETTINGS**  section. Just look for the line " **Do you have a code?** ", and " **Tap to enter** ", and the code activation screen will pop up.

After the code is activated, you can check all your activated add-ons on your account details page. Just tap " **Show details** " under ACCOUNT SETTINGS, and scroll down to the  **PURCHASED ADD-ONS**  section. If you have an active MysterSmith subscription, you will have access to all the available add-ons.
